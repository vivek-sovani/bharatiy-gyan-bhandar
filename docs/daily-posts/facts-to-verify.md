Where a day says "from the source", the claim comes from the site's own page text, so the page itself may need the same correction.

## D. Differences between the posts and the site pages

- **Day 79:** the site's Pañcatantra page says both "84 stories" and "eighty-odd". The post gives no number. The post also leaves out the page's claim that the Arabian Nights and the Decameron descend from the Pañcatantra, since that is contested.
- **Day 136, 137:** the Upaveda-to-Veda pairings follow the site, and sources vary.
- **Day 127:** the body-limb image of the Vedāṅgas (feet = chandas, hands = kalpa, eye = jyotiṣa, ear = nirukta, nose and mouth = śikṣā and vyākaraṇa) differs from the site's epithets (Śikṣā = mouth, Vyākaraṇa = face). The post notes that texts differ.
- **Day 155:** page count of The Life Divine differs inside the source.
- **Day 158:** the source's Columbia doctorate year may be wrong.
