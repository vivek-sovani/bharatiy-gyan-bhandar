### Full Description *(max 4000 characters)*

```
Bhāratīya Jñāna Bhaṇḍāra (भारतीय ज्ञान भण्डार) is a free, open digital library
of the texts, sciences and ways of living rooted in the Indic knowledge tradition.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• The Four Vedas — Ṛg, Yajur, Sāma and Atharva, the oldest surviving stratum of
  Indic thought, preserved as living sound before they were ever written.

• Upaniṣads — 108 meditative dialogues at the close of each Veda exploring the
  nature of the Self (Ātman) and the Absolute (Brahman).

• The Āstika Darśanas — six orthodox philosophical schools: Nyāya, Vaiśeṣika,
  Sāṅkhya, Yoga, Mīmāṃsā and Vedānta, together with their major sub-schools.

• Vedānta Schools — Advaita (Śaṅkara), Viśiṣṭādvaita (Rāmānuja) and Dvaita
  (Madhva), explained from first principles.

• Nāstika Darśanas — Cārvāka, Bauddha and Jaina, the heterodox schools that
  sharpen Indic philosophy by arguing against it.

• Itihāsa — the Rāmāyaṇa and Mahābhārata, including the Bhagavad Gītā.

• Purāṇas — Vaiṣṇava, Śaiva and Brahma Purāṇas with plot, cosmology and theology.

• Āgamas & Tantra — temple liturgy, mantra, yantra and the Kashmir Śaivism texts
  from Śiva-sūtra to the Vijñāna Bhairava's 112 meditations.

• Upavedas — Ayurveda (medicine), Dhanurveda (martial arts), Gāndharvaveda
  (music & dance) and Sthāpatyaveda (architecture).

• Six Vedāṅgas — the auxiliary sciences of phonetics, ritual, grammar, etymology,
  prosody and astronomy that make the Veda intelligible.

• Living Knowledge — Dinacharya (daily routine), Ṛtu-caryā (seasonal living),
  Pañcāṅga (traditional calendar), and more.

• Concepts glossary — key terms, core ideas and philosophical concepts explained
  in plain language.

FEATURES

✦ Three themes — cream, dark and saffron; your preference is remembered.
✦ Devanāgarī toggle — switch Sanskrit terms between transliteration and script.
✦ Bilingual content — English and Marathi (मराठी) throughout.
✦ Pañcāṅga — live traditional calendar with tithi, nakṣatra and yoga.
✦ No ads, no sign-in, no data collection. Works fully offline once loaded.
✦ Optimised for phones and tablets.

WHY THIS APP

India's knowledge tradition is vast, interconnected and largely inaccessible to
the general reader. This app presents it as one coherent library — not as
disconnected Wikipedia entries but as a living system, with the relationships
between texts, schools and disciplines made visible.

All content is original educational synthesis released under CC BY-SA 4.0.

---
An open-source project. Contributions welcome.
```

## Release Build Note

The current workflow produces a **debug APK** (`app-debug.apk`), which cannot
be published to the Play Store. For a production release you need:

1. **Generate a signing keystore:**
   ```bash
   keytool -genkey -v -keystore release.keystore \
     -alias bharatiyagyan -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000
   ```

2. **Add signing config to `android/app/build.gradle`** under `android { }`:
   ```groovy
   signingConfigs {
       release {
           storeFile file(System.getenv("KEYSTORE_PATH"))
           storePassword System.getenv("KEYSTORE_PASS")
           keyAlias System.getenv("KEY_ALIAS")
           keyPassword System.getenv("KEY_PASS")
       }
   }
   buildTypes {
       release {
           signingConfig signingConfigs.release
           minifyEnabled false
       }
   }
   ```

3. **Build an AAB** (Android App Bundle — preferred by Play Store):
   ```bash
   cd android && ./gradlew bundleRelease
   # Output: android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab
   ```

4. Store the keystore securely — losing it means you can never update your app
   on Play Store.
